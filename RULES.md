# Slow-Carb Diet (SCD) rules

This is the active reference for classifying food-log entries. It separates Tim Ferriss's canonical rules from the owner's intentional personal protocol. When they differ, use the personal protocol for the log and name the difference plainly.

## Canonical Ferriss rules

1. **No white or potentially white starchy carbohydrates** `[canonical]`
   - **Not allowed:** bread, pasta, rice (including brown rice), potatoes, cereal, tortillas, breading, and grains.
   - **Allowed foundation:** protein, legumes/beans, and non-starchy vegetables.
2. **Eat the same few meals repeatedly** `[canonical]`
   - Especially repeat dependable breakfasts and lunches. This is a simplicity rule, not a requirement that every meal be identical.
3. **Do not drink calories** `[canonical]`
   - Default to water and unsweetened, calorie-free drinks. Ferriss's stated exception is 1-2 glasses of dry red wine per night.
4. **Do not eat fruit** `[canonical]`
   - Tomatoes and avocado are Ferriss's stated exceptions.
5. **Take one day off each week** `[canonical]`
   - Ferriss chooses and recommends Saturday.

Ferriss also promotes **30 g of protein within 30 minutes of waking** as a Slow-Carb/default fat-loss habit, including in his own later writing. It is **not one of the five basic rules above**, and it is not active in the owner's protocol.

## Owner's active protocol

- **Run dates:** September 14, 2026 through March 14, 2027. `[personal]`
- **Cheat day:** Saturday; treat it as compliant and do not break the streak. `[canonical choice + personal tracking rule]`
- **White carbs/grains:** none on regular days, including bread, rice, cereal, pasta, potatoes, tortillas, and breading. `[canonical, clarified]`
- **Fruit:** none except tomatoes and avocado. `[canonical]`
- **Dairy:** none on regular days. `[personal stricter rule]`
- **Plant milks:** none on regular days. `[personal stricter rule]`
- **Home meals:** vegetarian; eggs and soy are allowed. Meat may be considered only at restaurants. `[personal]`
- **Meal structure:** favor whole-food protein, legumes, and non-starchy vegetables; repeating a small set of meals is encouraged. `[canonical + personal]`
- **Morning timing:** no food before 8:00 AM. Breakfast comes after meditation and, on lifting days, after the workout. `[personal replacement rule]`
- **30-in-30 protein rule:** intentionally inactive. Do not score 30 g of protein within 30 minutes of waking as a pass/fail compliance check. `[personal deviation]`
- **Satiety:** do not white-knuckle hunger. Increase meal size before blaming willpower or defaulting to snacks. Space meals to make breakfast at/after 8:00 AM and lunch around noon workable without a snack. `[personal]`
- **Accountability:** classify every new food entry as **Compliant**, **Maybe**, or **Not compliant**. Be encouraging and direct about deviations; a slip means return to SCD at the next meal, with no shame. `[personal tracking rule]`

## Quick checklist for each entry

**Allowed on regular days**

- Eggs, soy, other compliant protein
- Beans and lentils
- Non-starchy vegetables
- Tomatoes and avocado
- Water and unsweetened calorie-free drinks
- Meat/fish only when eating at a restaurant

**Not allowed on regular days**

- Bread, rice, cereal, pasta, potatoes, tortillas, breading, or other grains
- Fruit other than tomatoes and avocado
- Dairy
- Plant milks
- Sweetened drinks or other liquid calories
- Meat/fish in home meals
- Food before 8:00 AM

**Do not flag**

- Not getting 30 g protein within 30 minutes of waking. That Ferriss habit was intentionally replaced by the owner's morning schedule.
- Saturday cheat-day foods. Log them, but the day remains compliant.
